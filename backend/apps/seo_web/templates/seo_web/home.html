Frequently Asked Questions about ProFinder Pakistan

Clear, factual information regarding how ProFinder connects consumers with independent service experts across Pakistan.

What is ProFinder and how does it work?
ProFinder is Pakistan's comprehensive local service marketplace directory. Users can search for verified doctors, electricians, lawyers, plumbers, carpenters, and civil engineers across Sindh, Punjab, KPK, Balochistan, and Islamabad. You can view complete professional qualifications, customer reviews, hourly rates, and portfolio photos, and then initiate direct bookings.
Does ProFinder charge booking or payment commissions?
No. ProFinder operates as a direct discovery and booking platform. Customers pay zero booking commissions to ProFinder, and in-app financial transactions are excluded. All payments are settled directly between customer and professional via Cash on Delivery (COD), direct bank transfer, or mobile wallets (JazzCash / Easypaisa).
How does ProFinder verify professionals?
Professionals submitted to ProFinder undergo identity review including national identification, qualification certificates, and approved work portfolio samples before being granted the "Verified" badge.
How can I book an urgent service in my city?
Select your city or province from the navigation menu, filter by your required profession or emergency service, inspect available specialist profiles, and submit a booking request. You will receive direct communication from the chosen technician.
